I'm Thu Trang Ha, also known as Paige. I'm currently pursuing a Master's degree in Local Development at the University of Padua, Italy. Before that, I studied International Relations at the Diplomatic Academy of Vietnam.

My academic interests revolve around development, power, inequality, migration, climate change, education, and the relationships between people and places. I'm particularly interested in how development is understood and practised, who gets to shape it, and whose perspectives are often left out.

Coming from Dien Bien, Viet Nam and studying development in Europe, I find myself thinking about the ideas and theories I encounter in class in relation to the realities I know from home. I'm curious about alternative ways of thinking about development and how different experiences, histories, and power relations shape the way we understand it.

This blog is a space where I document my learning journey, write reflections on things I read and discuss, and explore questions that stay with me after class. I hope to share these thoughts with friends, colleagues, and others who might find them interesting or have something to add.

Outside my studies, I enjoy photography and am currently exploring film photography. I also love motorbike rides through the mountains of northern Vietnam, where winding roads disappear into the fog and rain.
